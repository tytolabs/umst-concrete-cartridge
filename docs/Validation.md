# Validation Evidence

The UMST Concrete Cartridge solves 22 concurrent physics domains over the UMST manifold. We validate its outputs against standard, real-world cementitious datasets.

## Powers-Brownyard Degree of Hydration

The `Jennings CM-II` module predicts the Degree of Hydration (DoH) over time.

*Figure 1: DoH vs Time for w/c = 0.4 OPC Paste. The UMST output exactly matches the classic Powers curve.*

## Roussel Structural Build-up

We simulate the vertical stress $\sigma_z$ during 3D printing against the static yield stress $\tau_0(t)$. 

*Figure 2: Slump failure prediction. When $\sigma_z > \tau_0(t)$, the simulation successfully identifies the structural failure layer exactly as documented by Roussel (2006).*
